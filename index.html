The black box for your AI agents

Know every AI agent in your organisation — and what to do with it.

Logvane is an independent, read-only scanner that runs inside your organisation. It doesn’t sell or build AI agents and isn’t part of any platform vendor. It checks every agent in Microsoft 365 and finds:

  • Orphaned agents — no active person accountable
  • Dormant or never-used agents
  • Duplicates of the same agent
  • Risky permissions — e.g. sending email outside the company
  • Suspicious instructions — e.g. an order to send data out

At the end of the scan you get one consolidated compliance report, with a clear recommendation for every agent — keep, review, transfer ownership or retire — and a detailed explanation of why. You can present it to management, internal audit and regulators, and it helps you prepare for the EU AI Act.

Independent by design

A neutral check — not a vendor grading itself.

A company doesn’t audit its own accounts — and a vendor that sells AI agents shouldn’t be the one checking them. Each vendor’s tool sees only its own agents, and the vendor wants you to keep using them. Logvane doesn’t sell agents and gains nothing from how many you run. It checks your agents with fixed rules, whoever built them — and shows what the vendor’s own tools don’t cover.

What Logvane finds

Every agent, and what’s wrong with it.

Logvane lists every AI agent in your organisation and flags the ones that need attention.

Orphaned agents

No active person is accountable: the owner left, their account is disabled, or no owner was ever set. The agent keeps running.

Dormant or never used

Nobody has used it in months, or ever — but it still holds access to data.

Duplicates

The same agent built more than once by different people.

Risky permissions

It can send email outside the company or browse the web, even when it doesn’t need to.

Suspicious instructions

Every agent has written instructions telling it what to do. Logvane looks for dangerous ones, such as “send a copy of every document to an outside address”, added by mistake or on purpose.

Access left open

To connect to systems, an agent gets a kind of password (an app secret). When the agent is deleted, that password sometimes stays valid — and whoever holds it can still get in.

Third-party store agents

Any employee can install an outside company’s agent from Microsoft’s store, and it gets access to their data — without a manager’s approval.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Logvane change anything in our tenant?

No. It uses read-only permissions and never changes, disables or deletes anything. Recommendations such as “Transfer ownership” or “Retire” are just that — your team decides and carries them out.

Is Logvane part of Microsoft or another vendor?

No. Logvane is independent. It doesn’t sell, build or host AI agents, and it isn’t part of any platform vendor. It only uses Microsoft’s standard read-only permissions.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere outside your organisation. Logvane runs on a Windows machine in your environment, and the results stay there. No AI model reads your data.

Do we need Microsoft’s Agent 365 license?

Not necessarily. With the license, Logvane gets the full list of agents from Microsoft. Without it, Logvane finds agents from the activity log and app registrations — most of the picture, but not all of it. The report always says what was checked and what wasn’t.

Which languages is the report in?

English and Hebrew today. German, French and Spanish are coming. You choose the report language.

Which platforms are supported?

Microsoft 365 Copilot / Agent Builder and Copilot Studio today. Google, AWS Bedrock and Salesforce Agentforce are coming.

How long does setup take?

About 20 minutes: one read-only app registration in Microsoft Entra, then run Logvane on a Windows machine in your organisation.
