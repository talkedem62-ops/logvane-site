Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

Logvane is run by Tal Kedem, Israel. Contact: hello@logvane.net. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

The Logvane product

Logvane runs inside your own organisation and is read-only. Scan results stay on your systems. Logvane does not send your organisation’s data to us, and no AI model reads it.

Business contact data

To introduce Logvane to organisations that may need it, we may use business contact details that are publicly available: your name, job title, employer, and a work email address or contact route published by you or your organisation (for example a company website, LinkedIn, or a published article). We also keep what you send us when you reply.

  • Purpose: to contact you about Logvane, answer your questions, and run a pilot if you ask for one.
  • Lawful basis (UK / EU GDPR): our legitimate interest in offering a relevant business product to people in relevant roles. We send a small number of personalised messages, never bulk mailings.
  • Retention: if there is no reply or further contact within 12 months, we delete your details.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share your data. Our email provider (Namecheap Private Email) stores messages on our behalf.

Opting out

Reply “remove” to any message, or write to hello@logvane.net. We will stop contacting you. We keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so that we don’t contact you again.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, and you can object to its use at any time. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU you can complain to your local data protection authority.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
